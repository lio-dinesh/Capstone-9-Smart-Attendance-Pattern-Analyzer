# Privacy & Responsible Use Statement

**Project:** Smart Attendance Pattern Analyzer  
**Standard:** Privacy-by-Design Academic Intelligence Charter  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

---

## 1. Ethical Mission & Non-Punitive Educational Paradigm

The **Smart Attendance Pattern Analyzer** is engineered with an unwavering commitment to supportive, human-led educational intervention. Traditional attendance monitoring often devolves into punitive surveillance, wherein automated alarms trigger grade deductions, disciplinary notices, or student stigmatisation.

This platform rejects the surveillance paradigm in favour of **supportive academic stewardship**:
- **Inquiry Prompts, Not Accusations:** Anomaly flags generated by this system are strictly administrative prompts inviting empathetic mentor consultation. They represent statistical patterns, not conclusions about student character, diligence, or integrity.
- **Structural First, Individual Second:** Attendance drops frequently reflect systemic friction—such as 8:30 AM timetable clashes, inter-campus bus delays, adverse weather, or faculty recording backlogs—rather than individual student failure. Institutions are required to evaluate cohort-level timetable trends before initiating individual outreach.
- **No Automated Sanctions:** Under no circumstances may flags or metrics produced by this system trigger automatic grade penalties, loss of financial aid, expulsion proceedings, or automated disciplinary notifications.

## 2. Pseudonymisation & Data Protection Standards

### 2.1 Identifiers & PII Policy
1. **Opaque Student Keys:** The system ingests and stores only pseudonymous identifiers (e.g., `STU-0012`). Direct Personally Identifiable Information (PII)—including legal names, home addresses, phone numbers, government IDs, email addresses, and demographic attributes—is completely excluded from dataset schemas.
2. **Key Separation:** Any mapping between pseudonymous student keys and real-world student identities must reside in an institutionally controlled, encrypted Student Information System (SIS) accessible only to authorized academic advisors.
3. **Public Export Privacy:** Aggregated cohort reports exported from the platform contain solely summary-level statistics. Individual student keys and granular session logs are excluded from public reporting views.

### 2.2 Access Controls & Authorization Boundaries
In a production deployment, access to the Smart Attendance Pattern Analyzer must be strictly segregated across role-based boundaries:
- **Academic Mentors / Advisors:** Authorized to view student-level streaks and record contextual notes following supportive 1-on-1 check-ins.
- **Course Instructors:** Authorized to view aggregated section trends and data completeness indicators for their own assigned courses; restricted from viewing individual students' cross-departmental records.
- **Department Chairs / Deans:** Access limited to anonymised, aggregated cohort and timetable analytics to inform scheduling and curriculum decisions.
- **Data Stewards / Administrators:** Authorized to manage ingestion pipelines, verify data hygiene, and resolve pipeline synchronization errors.

## 3. Data Integrity & The "Missing ≠ Absent" Protocol

A cornerstone of the platform's fairness architecture is the formal mathematical separation between recorded absences and missing entries:
- **Unknown Data Quality Gaps:** When an expected scheduled session held has no recorded attendance submission, the system categorizes the record as `MISSING`.
- **Prohibition of Silent Penalisation:** The calculation engine never converts missing records into absences. Silently treating missing entries as absences unfairly depresses student metrics due to instructor oversight or hardware malfunctions.
- **Provisional Status Safeguard:** If record completeness for any course or section falls below 85.0%, attendance percentages are prominently labeled **Provisional (Low Completeness)**, notifying decision-makers that the sample is insufficient for definitive academic analysis.

---

## 4. Auditability, State Preservation & Human Oversight

- **Preservation of Human Agency:** Academic advisors maintain final authority over all review flags. When a reviewer inspects a prompt and records that a student faced valid circumstances (e.g., verified illness or transit delay), the system persists this decision across all subsequent data re-ingestions.
- **Immutable Audit Trails:** Manual amendments to attendance records require an audit note explaining the justification. All edits record a timestamp and reviewer identity to prevent unrecorded record tampering.
- **Consent Assumptions:** Implementation assumes students provide informed consent during academic enrollment regarding the institutional use of attendance analytics for proactive student success and academic support initiatives.
